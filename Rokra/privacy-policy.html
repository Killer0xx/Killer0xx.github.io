ATMs near you, and whether they have cash

Rokra — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-20

Rokra ("the app", "we", "us") is a Pakistan app that tells you which ATMs near you have cash right now, from live reports by people standing at them, and shows those ATMs on a map. This policy explains what data the app collects, why, and how it's used. Rokra does not require an account or login.

Summary

  • No account, no login. Rokra does not ask you to sign up or sign in.
  • Location: used on your phone to show ATMs near you and to check that you are at an ATM when you answer "Is there cash?". It is sent to us only when you add a missing ATM (the ATM's position) or choose to send it with a "Wrong info?" report. It is never shown to other users and never sold.
  • Name and email: only collected if you choose to fill in the "Add a bank ATM" form. Used solely to process that request and, if needed, follow up with you about it.
  • Ads: the app shows ads (Google AdMob) to stay free. Ad providers may collect an advertising identifier as described below.
  • Rokra never guarantees that any ATM shown actually has cash — status is crowd-sourced and can be wrong or out of date.

1. Location data

Rokra uses your device's location to:

  • Show ATMs near you on the map.
  • Centre the map on your current position.

Location is used on your device to power these features. When you answer "Is there cash?" (or tap a quick update), the app first checks on your phone that you are within 100 m of the ATM. Your location is NOT sent with the report: other users only see the answer, the time and how many people said the same, under an id that is different at every ATM and cannot be traced back to you.

You can deny location permission; the app will ask you to choose a city instead and the map will still work, just without "near me" centring.

2. Name and email (Add a bank ATM form)

If an ATM or bank is missing from the map, you can ask us to add it using the "Add a bank ATM" form. This form asks for:

  • Your name (optional)
  • Your email address (required)
  • The bank name you're requesting

This information is sent to the app owner's backend and used only to process your request and reply to you if needed. It is not used for marketing and is not sold or shared with third parties, other than the infrastructure used to run the backend itself (Google Sheets / Google Apps Script, used only to receive and store these requests).

2a. Adding a missing ATM (Add ATM screen)

If you add a missing ATM, the app sends: the bank, the ATM's location (your GPS position while standing at it, and its accuracy), whether it is inside a branch or off-site, the ATM's full address as you type it, the app language and version, and a random app token (not linked to your name, number or account). The token is stored only as a one-way hash, so we can show you the review state of your own additions under Profile -> My added ATMs and limit misuse (5 per day).

For some additions the app may ask for one photo of the ATM machine or the bank's sign. This is optional. Before sending, the app removes all hidden photo data (EXIF: GPS position, phone model, time) and shrinks the picture. Please do not photograph people or faces. Photos are stored privately in the app owner's Google Drive and used only to check the ATM, never published and never shared. They are deleted after the review on request.

The owner checks each addition by hand. For a branch ATM far from every known ATM, the location is also compared with OpenStreetMap (roads and shops nearby). No reason for a decision is shared, and your additions are never shown to other users with your name (there are no names in Rokra).

2b. Quick updates and "Wrong info?" on an ATM card

Quick updates ("Long queue", "Card stuck"...) need you to be within 100 m of the ATM, like a cash report. Other users see only "A Rokra user", the time and how many agree: never a name, phone id or your location. Each update is stored with an id that is different at every ATM, so updates cannot be linked to one person. "Wrong info?" reports go only to the app owner; your location is sent with it only if you tick "send my location".

"Move the pin here" and "Is the pin right at this ATM?" send the place where you are standing (from your GPS) to the app owner, with the ATM it is about. When the owner approves it, or two people send the same spot, that ATM's pin moves there for every user. The pin shows only the ATM's position: never who sent it.

2c. Rokra ID and the leaderboard

Your Rokra ID (for example RK-1234567) is a number made from the random app token above. It is not your name, phone number or device ID, and a new one is made if you reinstall the app. It is shown on your Profile and Settings screens, sent with Help & Support messages so we can find your records, and shown on the public leaderboard next to the number of ATMs and fixes of yours that we approved. No name, photo or location is ever shown on the leaderboard.

2d. Help & Support messages

If you send us a message from Settings -> Help & Support, the app sends the topic you chose, your message, the app language and version, and your Rokra ID. We use it only to answer you and improve the app.

2e. Notifications

Kaju's reminders (salary day, Eid, "your ATM was approved") are scheduled on your phone. Nothing about them is sent to us. You can turn them off in Settings.

2f. Services that help run Rokra

  • Google Firebase (anonymous sign-in and Firestore) stores cash reports and quick updates. The anonymous sign-in has no name, email or phone number.
  • Google Apps Script, Sheets and Drive receive the forms above (Add ATM, Wrong info?, Help & Support, Add a bank ATM) for the app owner.
  • OpenFreeMap serves the map images (OpenStreetMap data). Like any website, its servers see your internet address when your phone downloads the map.
  • Google AdMob shows ads (section 3).

3. Advertising (AdMob)

Rokra shows ads through Google AdMob to remain free to use. AdMob may collect an advertising identifier and other technical data (such as device type and approximate location) to show and measure ads, and to limit how often you see the same ad. This is governed by Google's own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

You can reset or limit your advertising identifier in your device's system settings.

4. Crowd-sourced ATM data

Cash-status reports (whether an ATM has cash, is empty, etc.) are submitted by users of the app. Rokra combines these reports to show a status, but:

  • Statuses are estimates, not guarantees. Always have a backup plan for cash.
  • Reports are checked for basic plausibility (e.g. was the reporting device actually near the ATM) but Rokra cannot verify every report is accurate.

5. Data we do not collect

Rokra does not collect your name, phone number, contacts, photos from your gallery, or any other personal data beyond what's described above (the only photo is the optional ATM photo you take yourself, section 2a). There is no account system, so there is nothing tied to a persistent identity beyond the anonymous, random identifiers described in this policy.

6. Data retention and deletion

  • "Add a bank ATM" form submissions (name, email, bank) are kept only as long as needed to process the request, and are deleted on request (see Contact below).
  • Cash-status reports are not linked to a name or email, so there is no personal data to delete beyond the report content itself, which can also be removed on request if you can identify which report was yours (e.g. by time and ATM).

7. Children

Rokra is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date at the top will change. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data, or to ask us to delete something: In the app, open Settings → Help & Support → "Send us a message".
